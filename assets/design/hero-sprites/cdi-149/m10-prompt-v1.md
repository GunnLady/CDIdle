## Prompt exact

> Create one full-body 2D fantasy RPG warrior combat-idle sprite on a
> transparent background. Reference 1 is the authoritative M10 neutral
> character: preserve exactly the same identity, face, dark-brown hairstyle
> with long braid, warm light skin, olive-green scarf/cape and round clasp,
> dark gray horizontally banded chest armor with brown buckles,
> olive-and-cream split tunic panels, brown trousers, knee plates, bracers,
> and strapped brown boots. Do not redesign, simplify, recolor, add, remove,
> or swap outfit elements. Preserve the same body build and proportions.
> Reference 2 is only the approved asymmetric dual-wield combat-guard logic:
> feet staggered and grounded, knees slightly bent, torso alert, one weapon
> forward protecting the centerline and the other ready near the shoulder;
> use it for natural arm, elbow, wrist, and hand relationships, not for
> identity, clothing, swords, or proportions. Reference 3 is only the
> approved visual language for axe construction and rendering: forged
> single-edged steel head, straight wooden haft, believable head-to-handle
> junction, material detail and crisp fantasy-game illustration style. Equip
> M10 with exactly two matching short one-handed battle axes, not swords, not
> double-headed axes, not polearms, not oversized axes. Each axe has one
> compact single cutting edge and a straight short haft suitable for dual
> wielding. Keep both hafts geometrically straight; align each axe head
> correctly with its haft and each blade plane correctly with its gripping
> hand. Both hands must grip naturally with straight believable wrists and
> correct fingers; no twisted wrists, broken anatomy, duplicated fingers,
> fused hands, floating weapons, bent blades, warped shafts, or impossible
> angles. The forward axe is held low-to-mid in front to cover the centerline;
> the rear axe is raised beside and slightly behind the shoulder, ready but
> not mid-swing. Experienced warrior, serious focused facial expression, eyes
> tracking an opponent, combat-ready but no attack in progress. Full head,
> braid, cape, body, boots, both complete axes and all weapon tips inside the
> canvas with generous padding; the canvas may be wider if required and must
> never bend or wrap weapons to fit. Transparent alpha background only; no
> colored backdrop, scenery, ground, shadow plate, aura, magic, motion trails,
> text, labels, UI, border, or watermark. Match the established polished
> hand-painted anime/fantasy RPG sprite style of the references.
