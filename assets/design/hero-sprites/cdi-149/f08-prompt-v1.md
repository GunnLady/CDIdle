## Prompt transmis

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: 2D game character combat-idle sprite source

Primary request: Generate one new full-body combat-idle illustration of the
exact female warrior from Image 1. Preserve her identity, face, skin tone,
high gathered long fine box braids, burgundy layered plate/lamellar armor,
purple padded high-collar underlayer, shoulder plates, belt, segmented skirt
plates, dark trousers, thigh armor, round knee plates, strapped boots, colors,
materials and all outfit details. Change only her pose, facial expression and
combat equipment as specified below.

Input images:
- Image 1 is the strict authority for F08 identity, outfit and body proportions.
- Image 2 is only a pose reference for a credible high one-handed guard, natural
  armed wrist and elbow alignment, staggered combat stance, and active empty
  off-hand. Do not copy its man, clothing, colors, saber or exact silhouette.
- Image 3 is only the authority for the compact single-crescent axe head,
  compact poll/collar, straight short wooden haft and visual rendering. Do not
  invent a second axe.

Subject and expression: same woman as Image 1, with a focused, serious combat
expression, mouth closed and eyes fixed toward the opponent. She is an
experienced warrior ready to fight, not posing casually.

Weapon: exactly one practical compact one-handed battle axe in her right hand.
It has one single crescent steel cutting blade, a compact poll and collar, and
a short straight wooden haft with a wrapped grip. The axe must remain clearly
one-handed and much smaller than a greataxe. No second blade, no spike, no
second weapon and no shield.

Pose: three-quarter full-body combat guard with staggered feet, flexed knees
and a stable lowered center of gravity. Hold the right-hand axe high beside the
right shoulder, not behind the head and not resting on the shoulder, armor,
hair or braids. The axe head faces forward and slightly outward toward the
opponent so the cutting edge—not the flat—would lead a strike. The short haft
is rigid, perfectly straight and aligned through the closed right hand. Keep
the right wrist neutral and the elbow naturally bent and slightly lowered.
The left hand is empty, fully visible and active at low-to-mid height near the
centerline, in a relaxed guarding fist or open combat hand; it must not dangle.

Proportions: preserve Image 1 anatomy and match the established female Mage
F06/F08 template proportions for head, shoulders, trunk, arms, hips and legs.
The combat stance may widen the silhouette but must not shorten, enlarge or
warp the anatomy. Preserve the long-braid volume without enlarging the skull.

Style and framing: polished stylized 2D fantasy game character concept matching
Image 1 and the validated CDI-149 sprite family. Full character and complete
axe visible with generous transparent padding on every side. Genuinely
transparent background. No scenery, floor, cast shadow, text, logo or watermark.

Constraints: one character only; exactly one axe; correct hands and fingers;
straight haft; coherent axe-head angle; natural wrists and elbows; complete
weapon; complete boots; focused expression.

Avoid: dual axes, shield, dagger, saber, sword, giant or two-handed greataxe,
double-bit axe, rear spike, reverse grip, axe behind the head, blade threatening
the face or hair, flat of the blade leading the strike, bent or warped haft,
cropped equipment, forced perspective, oversized head, shortened trunk or
legs, extra fingers, smile, background scene, lettering, watermark.
