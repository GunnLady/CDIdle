# Header menu center v2

Reworked stretchable center section for the resource header. The approved lion
menu ornament was supplied directly as the authoritative metal reference.

The horizontal planks were darkened to deep aged walnut and given restrained
wear, stronger grain and uneven stain. The narrow top and bottom contours were
harmonized with the lion ornament's metal. Geometry remains straight and free
of end caps or baked functional content.

The generated chroma-key source was converted to alpha and normalized to a 4x
`1024 x 912` version of the original runtime proportions, including the same
visible-panel height and vertical placement. The metal bands were selectively
cooled and desaturated during processing so their weathering survives final
browser scaling without turning olive.
