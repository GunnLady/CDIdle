# Account button v2

Color harmonization of the approved crowned account button. The approved lion
menu ornament was supplied directly as the authoritative metal reference.

Only the overall forged-iron color was adjusted. The button silhouette,
dimensions, bevels, hammered texture, relief, lighting and crowned account
emblem remain unchanged. The generated chroma-key source was converted to alpha
and cropped to its visible bounds with eight pixels of padding.

The artwork remains presentation-only: interaction, accessible naming, focus,
hover and active states remain DOM/CSS.
